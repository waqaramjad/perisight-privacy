PeriSight AI Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026.

The short version

  • The camera picture and the depth sensor are used live on your iPhone, then thrown away. No picture is saved or sent.
  • There is no account. The app does not know your name.
  • The app sends anonymous usage counts and crash reports to Google Firebase, so we can see what to fix and improve.
  • Your feedback is sent only when you write it and tap Send.
  • The app never sends a picture, a sound, what you say or where you are.

1. Who makes PeriSight AI

PeriSight AI is an iPhone app for people with low vision, such as retinitis pigmentosa. It helps you walk, find things, explore a new place and name colours.

PeriSight AI is made by Waqar Amjad. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Waqar Amjad.

2. What stays on your iPhone

  • The camera picture and depth. The app uses them to draw lines and boxes and to warn you. Each frame is then thrown away. It is never saved and never sent.
  • Recognition. Finding the path, objects, colours, the road and the footpath all happens on the iPhone. So does speech recognition for “Find Eye”.
  • Your settings. Your choices, your calibration, and your Find favourites and recent searches are stored on the iPhone. They are also in your own iPhone backup, if you use one. Section 3 lists what is counted when you use them.
  • Feedback waiting to send. If you send feedback without a connection, it waits on the iPhone and goes when there is one.

Deleting the app deletes your settings and anything still waiting to be sent.

3. What is sent to Google Firebase

The app uses three Google Firebase services. Nothing sent carries your name or a picture. The app declares this data to Apple as not linked to you and not used for tracking.

Usage counts (Google Analytics for Firebase)

These tell us which parts of the app are used. The app sends:

  • which screen or tab is open, and for how long;
  • which button is tapped, and for a switch, whether it was turned on or off;
  • which setting changed, and its new value;
  • which kinds of thing you ask Find to look for, as names from the app’s own list (such as “key” or “glasses”), never the words you typed or said;
  • the kind of voice command used (such as “find” or “switch tab”), never the words;
  • when Siri or a shortcut opened the app on a tab;
  • problems: the phone too hot, a low battery, a tab that needs LiDAR, the microphone refused, or an error the app recovered from (where it happened, and an error code);
  • whether the iPhone has a LiDAR sensor.

Firebase also adds basic facts about the phone and the app, such as the iPhone model and the iOS and app versions. It makes a random number for this copy of the app, so visits can be counted without knowing who you are. Google estimates the rough area, such as the country or city, from the internet connection. It is never a GPS position.

The app uses Analytics without Apple’s advertising identifier, and with advertising features turned off. Nothing is sent per camera frame: only when you do something, change screen, or something goes wrong.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, or hits an error it recovers from, it sends a technical report. The report shows which part of the app failed, the iPhone model, the iOS and app versions, the screen that was open, and the names of the last few events listed above.

Feedback you choose to send (Cloud Firestore)

In Settings, “Send feedback” sends the message you write, up to 2,000 characters. It is sent with the app version, the iPhone model, the iOS version, the tab that was open, whether the iPhone has LiDAR, whether VoiceOver was on, and the time it arrived.

Please don’t put your name, phone number or email in it. The app can only add a message. It cannot read, change or delete any feedback. Only we can read it, in the Firebase console.

4. What is never collected

  • Camera pictures, video or depth images.
  • Sound recordings, or anything you say.
  • Your location. This version of the app does not ask for or use it.
  • Your name, email, phone number, contacts or photos.
  • Apple’s advertising identifier.

There is no account, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites. We do not sell any data.

5. Permissions, and why

  • Camera. Needed for every tab: to see the path, find things, outline a new place and name colours. Pictures stay on the iPhone.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition. Asked for only when you tap the microphone on the Find tab, so you can say “Find Eye, my keys”. It listens only while the Find tab is open and the microphone is on. Speech is recognised on the iPhone only, and is never saved or sent.
  • Motion sensors. Used to know which way the phone is pointing. This stays on the iPhone.
  • Location. Not asked for and not used in this version.

You can change any permission in the iPhone’s Settings app, under PeriSight AI.

6. Other companies

Google runs Firebase: Analytics, Crashlytics and Cloud Firestore. Google handles this data for us under its own terms, on its servers, which may be outside your country. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Apple provides the iPhone’s speech recognition, which the app runs on the iPhone only. If you use Siri, for example “Find my keys with PeriSight AI”, Siri handles what you say under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The app receives only the thing to find.

No other company receives data from the app.

7. How long data is kept

  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Usage counts: for Google Analytics’ retention period, at most 14 months.
  • Feedback: until you ask us to delete it.
  • On the iPhone: until you delete the app.

8. Children

PeriSight AI is not made for children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and nothing it sends says who you are. If a child has sent feedback with personal details in it, ask us to delete it.

9. Deleting data and asking questions

To ask a question, or to ask us to delete feedback you sent:

  • in the app, go to Settings, then Send feedback; or
  • open an issue at github.com/waqaramjad/perisight-privacy/issues. Issues are public, so don’t put personal details in one.

Feedback does not carry your name, so tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we will delete it.

Usage counts and crash reports do not name you, so we cannot pick out yours. They are deleted on their own after the times above. The app has no switch to turn them off yet.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date, and the app’s release notes say so. Every earlier version stays visible in this page’s history on GitHub.
